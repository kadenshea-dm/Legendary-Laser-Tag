More Than Just a Game

We're here for the teamwork, the friendly rivalries, and the stories everyone tells after the final score.

Our Story

Neon Strike Laser Tag began with a simple idea from fictional founder Jordan Ellis: make it easier for people to put their phones away, get moving, and enjoy an adventure together. After organizing competitive game nights for friends, Jordan imagined a glowing arena where first-time players could feel welcome and experienced players could test their strategies.

Jordan teamed up with operations manager Maya Chen, a lifelong fan of team challenges, to shape that idea into Neon Strike. Their goal is to make every visit feel energetic, welcoming, and worth sharing with friends.

What We Believe

Laser tag is at its best when everyone gets involved. Winning can be exciting, but good communication, creative thinking, and cheering on your teammates make the experience memorable. We want players to leave smiling, no matter which team earns the most points.

The Neon Strike Difference

Teamwork First

Great teams share information, look out for one another, and celebrate everyone's contribution.

Fun for Every Skill Level

New to laser tag? You can learn as you go. Every round is a chance to build confidence and try a new strategy.

Memories That Last

From surprise comebacks to hilarious team names, we believe the best part of game night is sharing it.

Meet the Team

Jordan Ellis, Founder: The big-picture thinker who dreamed up the Neon Strike experience and loves a clever team strategy.

Maya Chen, Operations Manager: The organizer who believes a welcoming atmosphere and clear instructions help every player feel ready to compete.

Together, Jordan and Maya built the fictional Neon Strike concept around one idea: the best competition brings people closer together.
