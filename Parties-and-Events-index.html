Make It a Celebration

Turn a birthday, team outing, or get-together into an action-packed occasion with friendly competition and plenty to talk about afterward.

Choose Your Kind of Event

Every group has its own reason to celebrate. These fictional event options are designed to give your gathering a theme, a shared challenge, and a reason to get everyone involved.

Birthday Battle

Make the guest of honor captain of the crew. Choose team colors, come up with a memorable team name, and compete for bragging rights on the birthday leaderboard.

Great for: Friends, families, and birthday crews.

Team Challenge

Bring classmates, coworkers, or club members together for a friendly contest. Rotate team captains, practice communication, and see how your group handles the pressure.

Great for: Student organizations, work teams, and clubs.

Friends' Night Out

Swap the usual hangout for a little competition. Create rival teams, test different strategies, and decide who gets to claim the title of champion.

Great for: Friend groups and casual celebrations.

Planning Your Event

Start by deciding what you're celebrating and how many people you expect. Think about whether your group would enjoy a relaxed series of games or a more competitive team challenge. Sharing those details helps shape an event that fits your group.

For this fictional business concept, event organizers are encouraged to plan ahead, arrive together, and agree on a team name before the first round begins.

Ready to Rally Your Team?

Gather your group, pick a theme, and imagine the stories you'll tell after the final round. Neon Strike events are built around participation, teamwork, and friendly competition.
